# ACES JET Club website

The website of the JET Club at ACES Nursery, Primary and Secondary School, built by the club's members.

**See it live:** <https://acesjetclub.github.io>

## Put yourself on the website

### 1. Get the website (one copy for each of you)

1. On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Open your **Downloads** folder, right-click the ZIP, choose **Extract All**, then **Extract**.
   Never work inside the ZIP: your changes won't save.
3. Find the folder that has **index.html** inside. It may be one folder deeper.
4. Copy that folder to your **Desktop** and rename it `acesjetclub-` plus your first name, like `acesjetclub-amina`.
   Sharing a laptop? Your partner makes their own copy.

**No internet?** Copy the `acesjetclub` folder from your teacher's USB stick instead, then rename it the same way.

### 2. Open it

- **To see it:** double-click `index.html`. It opens in the browser.
- **To change it:** open the whole folder in **Sublime Text** (**File › Open Folder**), or in **HCODX** (**Open Folder**).

After every change: **Ctrl+S** to save, then **F5** in the browser to refresh.

### 3. Your missions

Look for the ✏️ marks: they show the parts you change.

- ⭐ **Your card:** in `index.html`, press **Ctrl+F** and type `Student Card 1`. Change your first name and your class.
- ⭐⭐ **Your page:** open `pages/student-1.html`. Change your name, your class, and what you want to become.
- ⭐⭐⭐ **Things you like:** one `<li>` for each thing, as many as you want.
- Don't forget the ✏️ **title** at the very top of `student-1.html`: it's the name on the browser tab.

Only edit **Student Card 1** and **student-1.html**. The other cards are added by the computer.

### 4. The rules

- **First name only**, and nothing about where you live: this website is public.
- **No photos yet.** They're added later, only with your parents' OK.
- Change the words **between** the tags, never the tags themselves.

### 5. Stuck?

- **Nothing changed?** Did you save (Ctrl+S) and refresh (F5)? Are you working in your Desktop folder?
- **Page looks broken?** Press **Ctrl+Z** until it's fixed, then save again.

### 6. Hand it in

Save everything and close the editor. Check your folder has your first name on it, then copy it onto your teacher's USB stick.

## What's in this folder

| Folder or file | What it is                                                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `index.html`   | The home page                                                                               |
| `pages/`       | Each member's own page                                                                      |
| `styles/`      | The CSS: colours, sizes and layout                                                          |
| `scripts/`     | The JavaScript: the photo slider and the member cards                                       |
| `images/`      | The school photos, icons and logo                                                           |
| `vendor/`      | Fonts and code made by other people, so the site works without the internet. Don't edit it. |
