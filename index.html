About ACES

At ACES Nursery, Primary and Secondary School, education is about more than what students learn from textbooks. It is about developing confidence, encouraging curiosity, nurturing individual abilities, and helping every learner discover their potential.

We believe students should have opportunities to ask questions, explore their interests, express their ideas, and develop the skills they need to navigate an ever-changing world.

Where curiosity meets creativity

The JET Club is a student community within ACES dedicated to exploring science, technology, engineering, and creativity.

It provides an opportunity for students to go beyond theoretical learning and engage with ideas through practical activities, collaborative projects, experimentation, and presentations.

From discovering how technology works to building creative solutions, members are encouraged to think independently, work together, and develop the confidence to bring their ideas to life.
